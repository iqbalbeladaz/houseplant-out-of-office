## The entry

> Thank you for your email. I'm currently wilting. Your message is important to me — unlike me to my owner.

One line, 105 characters (gate: 1–120).

## Why this line should win

- **Reads as a real out-of-office auto-reply:** it opens with the standard "Thank you for your email" and a status line, then twists it.
- **Actually funny:** the stock OOO reassurance — "your message is important to me" — is turned into an accusation against the owner.
- **The plant's own life drives the joke:** wilting, thirst, being ignored — the neglect is the punchline, not decoration.
- **Tight inside 120 characters:** no wasted words, and the twist lands in the final clause.
- **Original:** no office cliches, no worn-out auto-reply phrasing.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. It is fully
deterministic: the same bytes on every run, no input, no randomness, no
network.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
